# Release 7.4.2 — Competency radar visibility and bilingual STAR evidence

## Corrections

- Removes the silent seven-axis truncation from the competency radar.
- Displays every active job requirement and every active competency added during the interview.
- Keeps a density notice when the radar contains more than ten axes instead of hiding data.
- Adds French and English detection for STAR evidence: situation, task, action, result, personal ownership and measurable outcomes.
- Normalises accents and straight/curly apostrophes before evidence analysis.
- Shows `not assessed` instead of a misleading `0%` when no candidate answer or interview evidence has been entered.

## Guardrails

- Role expectations remain fixed.
- Interview-added competencies remain explicitly identified and do not become job requirements.
- Official candidate score, official rank and canonical AI confidence are unchanged.
