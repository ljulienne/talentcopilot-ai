# Release 7.7.1 — Evidence Grounding & False-Positive Guardrails

## Purpose

Release 7.7.1 corrects false-positive transferable evidence introduced by the generic 7.7.0 engine. A candidate name, contact detail, location, employer, heading or isolated job title can no longer be presented as evidence of competence.

## Evidence contract

A related-evidence result now requires a complete source statement containing at least one of the following:

- a professional action linked to a relevant object or technology;
- a measurable professional result;
- a relevant certification, course or degree statement.

The service keeps the exact source excerpt. If no suitable statement is found, it returns `No direct evidence` and explicitly states that no sufficiently grounded related evidence was identified.

## Job-requirement guardrails

- Company-marketing sections and post-description promotional text are excluded.
- Degree, language and years-of-experience requirements remain eligibility checks.
- Generic headings are not promoted to technical requirements.
- Acronym and long-form duplicates are merged.
- Embedded catalogues generated by 7.7.0 are regenerated from the original offer.

## Safety

This release does not modify official fit scores, official ranks, canonical recommendations or saved human interview ratings.
