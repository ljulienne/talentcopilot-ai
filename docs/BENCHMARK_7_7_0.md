# Release 7.7.0 Multi-Domain Benchmark

## Covered scenarios

| Domain | Exact requirements checked | Capability checks |
|---|---|---|
| HRIS | SAP SuccessFactors, Power BI, AI | Core HR, interfaces, data quality, change, vendors |
| Software | Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD | APIs, testing |
| Finance | IFRS, SAP FI/CO, Power BI | consolidation, close, controls, budgeting |
| Sales | Salesforce CRM | enterprise sales, pipeline, forecasting, APAC |
| Supply chain | SAP MM, S&OP, Lean Six Sigma | inventory, logistics, suppliers |
| Marketing | SEO, Google Analytics 4, HubSpot, CRM | acquisition and conversion |

## Acceptance rules

- No HRIS requirement may appear in a non-HRIS benchmark.
- Exact named requirements must remain visible rather than being reduced to a generic category.
- Language and experience conditions must not become radar axes.
- Candidate evidence must distinguish exact use from related technology.
- Interview questions must be generated for an unknown technology from its kind, family and evidence status.
- The HRIS reference scenario must retain the validated 7.6 behaviour.
