# Release 7.8.1 — Recruitment Journey & Reporting

## Purpose

Complete the premium recruitment journey introduced in 7.8.0 by restoring essential navigation and reporting, making the candidate dashboard the natural post-analysis destination, and integrating compensation as an independent decision signal before and after interview.

## Delivered

- Permanent **Home** entry in the premium sidebar and a clickable TalentCopilot brand lockup.
- Automatic first-time navigation to **Dashboard Perspective** when candidate analysis is complete.
- Whole-pool Dashboard Perspective with candidate cards, filters, sorting and contextual drill-down to Candidate Intelligence.
- Five recruiter-facing destinations: Overview, Dashboard Perspective, Compensation & Budget, Interview & Assessment, Compare & decide.
- **Compensation & Budget** workspace for position budget, candidate salary and benefit expectations, availability, negotiation flexibility and offer scenarios.
- Separate Talent Fit and Compensation Fit signals; compensation never changes official scores or ranks.
- Restored PDF exports for recruitment overview, candidate perspective, individual candidate, interview assessment, compensation and final comparison.
- Sticky blue/cyan recruitment journey strip rendered before page-specific content.
- Stronger sidebar text contrast and weight for improved readability.
- Blue/cyan primary actions; red remains reserved for destructive or critical actions.
- Historical navigation labels retained as hidden compatibility routes.

## Governance

- Official candidate match scores, ranks, evidence classifications and pre-interview recommendations remain immutable.
- Interview findings and compensation feasibility remain separate, traceable decision signals.
- Recruiter-entered compensation data is stored in recruitment-session metadata with timestamped audit entries.
- PDF reports reproduce stored product data and do not recalculate candidate assessments.
