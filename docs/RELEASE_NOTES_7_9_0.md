# TalentCopilot-AI 7.9.0 — Premium UX Consolidation

## Purpose

Release 7.9.0 consolidates the Recruitment experience into one coherent premium interface. It changes presentation, navigation density and progressive disclosure only. It does not change matching, ranking, evidence, interview assessment or compensation engines.

## Experience improvements

- A compact sticky workflow rail remains at the top of every Recruitment page.
- Recruitment pages share the same compact header, metadata and status pattern.
- The sidebar is narrower, denser and higher contrast.
- Dashboard Perspective defaults to a compact candidate list and retains an optional card view.
- Candidate filters, sorting and contextual drill-down remain available.
- Portfolio charts are divided into essential visuals and on-demand advanced analytics.
- Candidate Intelligence uses one visible continuation action instead of duplicate interview CTAs.
- Loading skeletons and compact empty states provide clearer intermediate feedback.
- Existing PDF exports remain visible in Recruitment Overview, Dashboard Perspective, Candidate Intelligence, Interview, Compensation and Compare & Decide.

## Preserved contracts

- Official match scores and ranks are not recalculated.
- Candidate-specific strongest areas and primary risks remain grounded by Release 7.8.2.
- Compensation Fit remains independent from Talent Fit.
- Candidate, mission, filters and workflow context remain preserved across navigation.
- Candidate Intelligence and Interview retain their three guided sections.
