# Release 7.8.0 — Premium Recruitment Experience

## Purpose

Reduce navigation and information overload without changing official matching, ranking, evidence or interview assessment data.

## Delivered

- Modern dark sidebar with no radio circles or checkbox-like navigation.
- Four recruiter-facing destinations: Overview, Candidates, Interview, Compare & decide.
- Active mission context, useful badges and a state-aware recommended next action.
- Configurable Digital Synergy identity with the slogan **Human Intelligence. AI Amplified.**
- Central brand configuration and reusable SVG/PNG assets.
- Blue/cyan primary actions; red remains reserved for destructive or critical states.
- Candidate Intelligence consolidated into Overview, Competencies and Evidence.
- Interview Intelligence consolidated into Prepare, Conduct and Assessment.
- Advanced governance and repeated technical detail hidden behind progressive disclosure.
- Modern workflow status strip without selection circles.

## Governance

This release is presentation-only. It does not recalculate or mutate official candidate fit, rank, evidence classifications, competency assessments or final decisions.
